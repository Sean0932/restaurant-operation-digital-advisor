# Example: US Multi-Location Brand

User:

> We have 8 locations and want to expand to 20. Our POS is fine, but reporting, inventory, and labor scheduling are inconsistent by location. What should we fix first?

Expected handling:

- Use Expansion Readiness + Readiness Check.
- Prioritize scalable workflows and data definitions.
- Avoid generic growth advice.

Output points:

- Identify what is already repeatable and what depends on local manager habits.
- Standardize inventory count, waste, labor scheduling, and reporting definitions.
- Define expansion gate checks before adding locations.
