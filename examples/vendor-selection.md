# Example: Vendor Selection

User:

> We are comparing two POS + inventory vendors. Can you tell us which one to choose?

Expected handling:

- Do not rank by default.
- Ask for market, store count, budget, current stack, required modules, integration needs, and candidates.
- If candidates are provided, create a comparison matrix.

Demo scenarios:

- refund after payment,
- canceled delivery order after kitchen prep,
- inventory correction after stock count,
- supplier invoice mismatch,
- loyalty coupon issued online and redeemed offline,
- month-end reconciliation.
