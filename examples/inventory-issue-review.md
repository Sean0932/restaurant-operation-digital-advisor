# Example: Inventory Issue Review

Use this when the user says inventory is inaccurate.

Minimum questions:

- How many stores?
- Which system is the inventory source?
- Are receiving, transfer, waste, and stock count recorded in the same system?
- Does POS sales deduct stock or recipe ingredients?
- Who can correct inventory?
- When is the mismatch discovered?

Recommended first action:

Pick 10 high-frequency SKUs and compare:

- system stock,
- physical count,
- last 7 days of receiving,
- sales deduction,
- waste,
- transfer,
- correction records.
